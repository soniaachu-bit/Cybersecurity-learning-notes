# ISO 27001:2022

## What is ISO 27001?

ISO 27001 is an international standard of specific requirement  for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving information security management system (ISMS) in order to manage information security risk.


## What is an ISMS?

Information Security Management System is an organized  system of policies, processes, people, responsibilities, and control for managing an organization information security risks and continually improving it's security system.


## What is the difference between ISO 27001 and an ISMS?

	a. ISO 27001 is the requirement for managing information security risk while ISMS is the organized system an organization use in managing information security risk and in it's continuous improvement.
	b. ISO 27001 contains the requirements an organization must meet to confirm to the standard while ISMS involves the process, policies, people, responsibities and control an organization use to manage security risk.
	c. ISO 27001 is the standard while ISMS is the standard put into work. It's the established system by an organization in order to meet up with the ISO standard's requirements 
	

## Why is ISO 27001 considered risk-based?

ISO 27001 is considered risk based because it helps organizations identify potential security risk and determine the control measures to reduce or mitigate such risk.

Think of ISO 27001 as the roadmap on the kind of controls that can be put in place to reduce certain risk impact.


## What is a security control?

A security control is a measure used to prevent, detect or reduce the impact of a security risk.

It is the measures put in place to prevent, detect, control, and reduce the impact of security risk in an organization.

## What is the difference between risk assessment and risk treatment?

Risk assessment is assessing the organization information security for risk. It's like asking the difficult question of what can go wrong? 

While risk treatment is accepting, mitigating, managing or controlling the identified risk in an organization. It's like asking how the organization can either manage or eradicate the identified risk in an organization.

This includes: 
	- Mitigating or reducing the risk
	- Finding a way to avoid or prevent the risk
	- Transfer or sharing the risk
	- Accepting the identified risk as not all risk can be eliminated.


## What is the purpose of the Statement of Applicability (SoA)?

The purpose of Statement of Applicability (SoA) is to document the Annex A control that is applicable to an organization ISMS, whether it is implemented and the status associated with them.

It helps to answer questions like: Which control measure is an organization to use for an idenified risk? Why was the control measure chosen? It's implemention status.


## What is the difference between implementing ISO 27001 and being ISO 27001 certified?

Implementing ISO 27001 is when an organization establish, implement, maintain, and continuously improve their ISMS based on the ISO 27001 standard. While ISO 27001 certified involes an external certification or audit.

An organization can implement an ISMS based on ISO 27001 with necessarily being certified 

While ISO 27001 certification requires an external certification or audit. 


## What do you understand by Annex A Control?

Annex A is a set of controls that organization can consider when treating information security risk

Under the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A has 93 control divided into four sections 

	a. People control 
	b. Physical controls
	c. Technological controls
	d. Organizational controls.
