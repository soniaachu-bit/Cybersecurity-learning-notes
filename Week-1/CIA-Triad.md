#CIA Triad

This is the foundadtion of information security. It consists of confidentiality, intergrity, and availability. It is one of the fundamentals of IT security. It can also be referred to as "AIC"

#How it works

1. Confidentiality: This prevents discolusre of information or database to unauthorized individuals or system in or outside an organization (i.e: encryption, access control, 2FA/MFA).
2. Integrity: This is to ensure that every information or message passed cannot be modified without dictation (e.g: use of hasing sysytem, digital signatures, certificates, non-repudiation).
3. Availabilty: This is to ensure that systems and networks are always up and running without interferance. (i.e: redundacy, fault tolerance, patching, backup).

#Why is CIA important?

1. Without the introduction of CIA in an organization or country, impormant data and information can easily be gotten & modified thereby causing the organization or country and every other person affected to be in a state of vulnerability.
2. Without constant access to necessary data and systems by authorized users the productivity of an orginaztion can be slowed down.

#Key Takeaways
The CIA triad forms the foundation of cybersecurity. Every security control is designed to protect one or more of these three principles/framework.
