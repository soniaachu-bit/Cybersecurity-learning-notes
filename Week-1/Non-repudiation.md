# Non-repudiation
Non-repudiation is the act of ensuring a person cannot deny that they performed a specific action such as sending a message, approving a transaction, or siging a document. This can be done using cryptography (proof of intergrity or proof of origin with high assurance of authenticity).

# Why does it matter?
1. With non-repudiation, data or messages can be eaisly verified.
2. With non-repudiatin, data received from the sender remains accurate and consistent (proof of intergrity).
3. By using proof of origin (digital signature) to send an information, the sender's identity can be known or verified.

# How does it reduce risk?
1. By using proof of intergity (hash method), no modification of the data or information will go unnoticed.
2. You can easily verify a data or message received.
3. This reduces the risk of sending wrong information from a verified source.

Key takeways: Non-repudiation is done using cryptography. Think of non-repudiation as proof that an action was done by someone and cryptography the technology that creates the proof of this action.
